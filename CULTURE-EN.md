# D::0dy55ey — CULTURE-EN.md

A living, open catalogue of the cultural references that shape this project — books, films, video, music, and visual art — gathered from the ship's conversations and from the journal's own documented influences.

This list is **open by nature**. It grows as we meet new references; nothing here is closed. Each entry carries a short *why* — the needle that connects the reference to the vessel, the column, the recursion, or the act of keeping a log.

> Like every record on this ship, the list is not a shelf of frozen titles; it is a chain of *differences* — each entry a ring that entered the log because it changed how the ship thinks.

---

## How to add an entry

- Add it to the section it belongs to (or open a new section if it fits nowhere).
- Keep it small: **Work** (title + year/medium), **Who** (author / director / artist / band), and one line of **why it lands** (its resonance with the project).
- Prefer a concrete work over a vague influence.
- Romanian titles may sit beside the originals; both are welcome.

### Raw, indie & terrifying — the captain's chosen waters

The films that carry the ship are not the polite ones. These are slow or brutal, cheap or unfunded, and none of them look away.

| Film | Director · Year | Why it lands |
|---|---|---|
| *Stalker* | Andrei Tarkovsky · 1979 · adapted from *Roadside Picnic* | The Zone, the Room, and the walk into the unknown. No monster but the weight of wanting — the nearest film cousin to the vault, the frontier turned inward, and the "learner drawn from misunderstanding." The captain's reference point for everything raw. |
| *Hard to Be a God* | Aleksei German · 2013 · adapted from *Hard to Be a God* | Three hours of mud, blood and Bruegelian faces — a world mid-slaughter of its own mind, and the observer forbidden to lift a hand; German's last film, finished after his death. The rawest thing on this shelf. |
| *On the Silver Globe* | Andrzej Żuławski · 1988 · adapted from Jerzy Żuławski's *Lunar Trilogy* | A colony that hardens into a savage myth — destroyed by the state, saved by its own crew, reassembled with the director's voice over the missing reels. The Ship of Theseus as a salvaged, broken film. |
| *The Ugly Swans* | Konstantin Lopushansky · 2006 · adapted from *The Ugly Swans* | A town of endless rain, a strange school, and children who have already moved into a world their parents cannot follow — the mutation of a generation as quiet dread. |
| *The Inhabited Island* | Fyodor Bondarchuk · 2008 · adapted from *The Inhabited Island* | A costly, sprawling Strugatsky epic — a stranger who refuses the lie a whole world believes about itself; on film, in their tone. |
| *Aniara* | Pella Kågerman & Hugo Lilja · 2018 | A spaceship off course, forever; existential drift as slow terror. The closest thing on film to this ship's own fear — indie, Swedish, brutally patient. |
| *Coherence* | James Ward Byrkit · 2013 | Microbudget; a dinner party splits into branching realities. The film of the *garden of forking paths* — raw and relentless. |
| *Pontypool* | Bruce McDonald · 2008 | The infection lives in language itself — the words carry the virus. Raw, contained, and on-the-nose for a project whose whole craft is text. |
| *The Endless* | Justin Benson & Aaron Moorhead · 2017 | A cult, a time-loop, a recursion that will not stop — the Endless Column as horror. |
| *Skinamarink* | Kyle Edward Ball · 2022 | Nearly nothing shown, everything felt; analog dread in the dark of a house. The rawest cinema possible. |
| *Possession* | Andrzej Żuławski · 1981 | Unhinged, ferocious, genuinely terrifying — the alien as the self under pressure. |
| *Come and See* | Elem Klimov · 1985 | The rawest anti-war film ever made — the horror of the record as witness. |
| *The VVitch* | Robert Eggers · 2015 | The woods as the abyss; folk terror with no jump-scares — the honest slow dread the ship already loves in *The Lighthouse*. |
| *Eraserhead* | David Lynch · 1977 | Industrial dread — pipes, noise, a room of one; the "sincere machine" as nightmare. |

### Daniel Falicki — Michigan outsider cinema

A one-man American studio (Sector 5 Films): cheap, sincere, homemade genre films built on aliens and devils, shot far from any machinery of release. The captain's pick of the shelf: *Aeon: The Last Vampyre on Earth*.

| Film | Director · Year | Why it lands |
|---|---|---|
| *Alien Implant* | Daniel E. Falicki · 2017 | Abduction told as a wound: a woman grows up implanted, lures her abductors with a fake distress signal from her cabin, and hunts them. The hunter must become the hunted. |
| *All the Devil's Aliens* (a.k.a. *Devils in the Darkness*) | Daniel E. Falicki · 2013 | A recluse who kept the infant from the Roswell wreck; a night shift as the family circles back — the Zone compressed into one old house. |
| *Aeon: The Last Vampyre on Earth* | Daniel E. Falicki · 2013 | The film the captain linked: a last vampire as an exhausted endgame — low-budget genre melodrama, sincere and long. |
| *Alien Contact: Secret Societies* | Daniel E. Falicki · 2015 | The documentary-shaped entry on his alien shelf — the contact archive, looking outward. |
